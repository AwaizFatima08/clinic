Privacy Policy

Clinic Schedule website (clinic-schedule.homilabs.org) and Android app (org.homilabs.clinicschedule). Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Who we are

Clinic Schedule is an appointment booking service for a single clinic, built and run by HomiLabs for that clinic. The clinic decides how appointments are handled; HomiLabs operates the system. Questions about this policy or your data: support@clinic.homilabs.org.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, phone / WhatsApp number, email addressTo create your account, identify your booking and contact you about it.
PasswordTo log in. It is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; the clinic never sees it.
Reason for the visit (optional, typed by you)So the doctor can prepare. This may be health information; only give what you are comfortable sharing.
Appointments: date, time, booking reference, status (booked, cancelled, completed, missed)To run the clinic's schedule and send you confirmations and reminders.
Notifications and emails sent to you; account created and last login timesTo show your messages in the app and keep the account working.
IP address (briefly, in memory)To limit repeated attempts and protect the service from abuse. It is not stored with your account.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, we do not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own account, appointments and notifications.
  • Clinic staff (admin accounts) see the appointments and the details above so they can manage the schedule.
  • Service providers who process data for us only to run the service:
    • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) stores the account and appointment data on servers in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. See Firebase privacy information.
    • Hostinger hosts this website and the clinic mailbox that sends confirmation and reminder emails.

We share data with others only if the law requires it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read only their own data; all changes go through the clinic's server functions.

How long we keep it

  • Your account (name, phone, email, login) is kept until you delete it.
  • Appointments stay linked to your account while it exists. When you delete your account, the clinic keeps only the date, time and outcome of past appointments, with your name, phone, email and visit reason removed.
  • Bookings made without an account (on the website or by the clinic for you) are kept as clinic records. Ask us at the address above to remove your details from them.
  • Emails and staff notifications that were already sent (for example "patient cancelled") may still contain your name in the clinic's mailbox or notification list.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app (My appointments → Delete my account) or by email. Full steps and what is deleted: Delete your account.

Children

The service is meant for adults. A parent or guardian should book for a child, using their own account.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it by writing to support@clinic.homilabs.org. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
