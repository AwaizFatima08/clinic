Delete your Clinic Schedule account

For the Clinic Schedule Android app (org.homilabs.clinicschedule) and the website clinic-schedule.homilabs.org, run by HomiLabs.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Clinic Schedule app and log in.
  2. Go to My appointments and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account, read the summary and tap Delete account.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out.

Option 2 — by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to support@clinic.homilabs.org with the subject Delete my account, from the email address you log in with. Include the phone number on your account. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done. We may ask a question to make sure the request really comes from you.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password).
  • Your profile: name, phone number and email.
  • Your in-app notifications and the emails we sent you that are stored in our system.
  • From all your appointments: your name, phone, email, visit reason and the link to your account.
  • Upcoming appointments are cancelled, so the time becomes free for other patients. You receive one last cancellation email, and the clinic is told the slot is free.

What the clinic keeps

  • For past appointments: only the date, time, booking reference and whether the visit took place, cancelled or was missed — with no name or contact details. The clinic needs this for its schedule and visit history.
  • Messages already sent to clinic staff (for example "patient cancelled") may still show your name in the clinic's notification list or mailbox.
  • Any medical notes the doctor keeps outside this booking system are not part of this app; ask the clinic directly about those.
